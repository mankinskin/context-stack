## Acceptance Criteria

1. A characterization document enumerates every kernel module's
   responsibilities and its inbound/outbound coupling to other modules.
2. The document classifies each cross-module re-export as structural or
   compatibility-shim.
3. No crate-split or restructuring waypoint (tracked separately) proceeds
   ahead of this document being complete and linked back to this ticket.
4. A documented decision exists on whether the first spec for this surface
   is authored now or deferred, with rationale.
