## Acceptance Criteria

1. Every restructuring change traces to a specific finding recorded in
   ticket 7205c1e1's characterization document.
2. The benchmark suite from ticket d320e80d is re-run before and after the
   restructure, and any regression is either resolved or explicitly
   documented with rationale.
3. No module is split into its own crate unless the characterization
   document identifies that boundary as load-bearing.
